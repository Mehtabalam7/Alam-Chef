# **App Name**: Culinary Maestro

## Core Features:

- Chef Profile & Biography: A dedicated section showcasing the chef's 24+ years of experience, culinary philosophy, and career highlights.
- Cuisine Specialization Showcase: Detailed sections for each cuisine expertise (e.g., Indian Tandoor, Chinese), highlighting unique skills and signature styles within each.
- Dish Portfolio Gallery: High-quality visual gallery of the chef's signature dishes and culinary creations across various cuisines, with filtering options.
- Testimonials and Accolades: Display positive reviews, professional recognition, and awards received throughout the chef's career.
- AI Dish Description Assistant: An AI tool that generates evocative and professional descriptions for dishes based on key ingredients or cooking style, aiding the chef in presenting new creations.
- Contact & Booking Inquiry: A streamlined form for potential clients, restaurants, or event organizers to contact the chef for bookings or collaborations.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A rich, earthy dark brown (#66401A) evoking warmth, grounding, and the sophistication of culinary excellence, chosen for its professional depth.
- Background color: A very dark, subtle brownish-grey (#1D1A16) to provide a luxurious, deep canvas for content, maintaining readability and elegance in a dark scheme.
- Accent color: A vibrant yet refined red (#E05252) used sparingly for calls to action and highlights, adding a pop of energy and passion without overpowering the primary palette.
- Headlines font: 'Playfair' (serif) for a high-end, elegant, and artistic feel, reminiscent of classic fine dining. Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for clean readability and modern professionalism in descriptive text.
- Utilize minimalist and sophisticated line icons for navigation and features, maintaining a clean aesthetic that supports the high-quality imagery.
- An elegant, photo-centric layout with generous white space (or dark space, in this scheme) to highlight the visual appeal of dishes. Clear, distinct sections guide the user through the chef's diverse portfolio.
- Subtle, smooth animations for transitions between sections and image loading, providing a fluid and premium user experience.
